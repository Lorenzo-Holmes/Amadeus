# Structural pre-display calibration decision

Task: G6-07-STRUCTURAL-PRE-DISPLAY-CALIBRATION-AND-NEW-CANDIDATE-V1.
Status: DESIGN ADOPTED FOR IMPLEMENTATION; effectiveness unproven; paid gate CLOSED.

Deterministic rules reliably check byte identity, exact quote locations, declared references, finite display boundaries, request counts, version bindings and allowed local edit application. They cannot establish general-language entailment, completeness of claim extraction, correctness of general knowledge, or absence of unsupported causal claims. Expanding a keyword detector cannot meet this task's coverage requirement.

A fixed second model stage can examine the complete eligible generation context and immutable initial draft for assertion strength, missing auxiliary premises and remaining alternatives. It may itself miss errors, introduce errors, damage useful certainty or degrade expression. Its audit labels and references are conversation-quality evidence, never truth certificates or state authority. Offline authored responses exercise the implementation only. A preregistered target-model smoke with separate internal review is necessary before formal allocation; no independent external reviewer has been established.

## Minimal extension and fixed disposal

One draft request uses the original single-call ProviderJournal and immutable raw storage. One separate versioned calibration journal records the second request under the same product turn and parent call identity; it does not create another product/user turn. Keep both original uniqueness constraints. Every response, including apparently correct drafts, enters calibration once unless a known hard stop or explicit input/identity failure already prevents it. No third stage, alternate candidate, readiness request or automatic retry exists.

Calibration returns a closed structured conversation-quality record. Exact quotations locate draft assertions and eligible-context references; the host resolves their exact Unicode spans. The record states modality, conditions, scope, quantifier, frequency, causal strength, missing premises, remaining explanations and assertion/quote/negation/code/hypothesis status. Ordinary knowledge and valid mathematical/conditional inference remain permitted with their basis identified. General knowledge cannot certify the current case's specific cause.

KEEP requires byte-for-byte original text. REVISE requires explicit localized edits anchored to identified overstatements; the host constructs the sole candidate by applying those edits and checks exact equality with the proposed final text. Unchanged regions remain byte-for-byte preserved. Unresolved findings, malformed/empty output, invalid references, overlapping edits, attempted extra calls, tampered identity or unsupported output contract produce HOLD. End disclaimers do not resolve an earlier located overstatement. HOLD does not expose the initial draft, issue a generic fallback, request repair or count as utility PASS.

New binding/record versions carry pipeline identity and both call identities. The display acknowledgment selects only the final accepted text. History, next context, evaluation, review and admitted speech observation consume that same acknowledgment. Internal drafts/critiques remain private audit objects. Admission and reducers remain unchanged: DISPLAY_ELIGIBLE != FACT_TRUE != STATE_ADMITTED != STATE_COMMITTED.

Submission intent precedes network I/O. Ambiguous outcome stops the whole relevant scope; reuse returns existing evidence without resubmission. Display intent without acknowledgment remains ambiguous and is never automatically replayed. After acknowledgment, only the original idempotent speech-observation commit may resume. Context/draft budget excess explicitly holds; neither is truncated to fit the new stage.

Separate counters report product turns, draft requests, calibration requests, total Provider requests, usable final answers and reviewed original criteria. Smoke maximum 50 requests; formal maximum 88; task maximum 138. All stages share the exact authorized model, route, transport and accounting controls, with stage-specific preregistered input limits. Offline preflight checks both stages with network disabled.

Before the first paid request: freeze implementation/configuration, independent smoke inputs/order/criteria/thresholds/reviewer method, request/fee caps and stops. Do not adjust these after observing paid output. Smoke failure or unresolved quality ends this paid cycle without a formal draw. Authored-draft probes remain distinct from end-to-end model-generated drafts and cannot estimate natural-draft improvement when the latter have no observed defects.
